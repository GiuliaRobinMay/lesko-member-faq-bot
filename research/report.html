Billing is the whole ballgame. Cancelling and money questions together are 58 of 148 posts — 39% of every practical question asked, more than the next three themes combined. A bot that answers only those two well already removes two of every five support posts.

In members' own words

Verbatim posts from the Questions Channel, one cluster per top theme. These are the phrasings the bot has to recognise — misspellings, all-caps frustration and one-line requests included.

Cancel my subscription

35 posts · rank 01

Almost never a complaint about content — it's people who cannot find the cancel control. Many have already tried email and got no reply, so they escalate publicly. The answer needs the exact click path, plus what happens to billing on the 5-day trial.

  • "How do I cancel my subscription?"
  • "Where is the link to cancel before the 5-day window?"
  • "I'm Changing Banks, How Do I Cancel My Paid Membership?"
  • "Can anyone tell me how to cancel my paid membership? Who can I email or call?"
  • "I have been trying to cancel my membership for two months now and can't seem to get $19 and something quit getting taken out of my account."
  • "It is next to impossible to cancel this subscription or speak to someone on here!"

Refunds, charges and payment method

23 posts · rank 02

Three recurring shapes: an unexpected renewal, a trial that converted, and a profile that says "Free Member" while the card is still being charged. Members also need a self-serve way to update an expired card.

  • "How do I get a refund?"
  • "Hello there, my accont auto renewed. How can I request a refund?"
  • "I'm being charged a monthly subscription yet on this website it says my membership is free. Something is not adding up."
  • "I joined for your dollar special and not only did you get your dollar you got $19.95. I did not authorize this amount."
  • "How do I update my payment card onfile"
  • "what is the 60.00 charge"

Live calls, Zoom links and replays

18 posts · rank 03

Time-critical and highly automatable: what's on today, the joining link, and where last week's recording lives. A recurring sub-case is Zoom itself failing — stuck in the waiting room, or no audio and video.

  • "How do I join zoom?"
  • "is there a zoom meeting today?"
  • "would like to get in on the 7;30 zoom meeting I signed up for tonight where can I find the meeting Id or the invitation link?"
  • "Did I miss the 10 PM credit repair workshop? … And how can I replay a video?"
  • "Im trying to get into a zoom class for tonight but zoom says that we are waiting on the Host"
  • "Tried to do a call sheet today, but the link doesn't work. Where do I get the call sheet?"

Personal Report, mini-500 and books

18 posts · rank 04

The Personal Report is a manual, staff-fulfilled item, so members chase it in public: how to request one, how to add topics, and whether theirs has been produced yet. Paid books and e-books that never arrived sit in the same cluster.

  • "How and who do I ask to start my personal report?"
  • "Can someone tell me how to get to where my personal report is?"
  • "Can you please update my comprehensive personal report?"
  • "I submitted the information requested for my Lesko Personal Report on 6/16/23. Please let me know if I should have received it or not by now?"
  • "I bought the E Book for Texas…and i do not see an email with it… Where is the booK"
  • "During onboarding, I paid an additional fee to receive a book. When will I receive the book?"

Navigating and finding things

17 posts · rank 05

The most emotionally loaded theme. Members describe the site as a maze of links that never resolve to the thing they were promised. This is the cluster where a bot that answers "where is X" in one hop replaces the most frustration.

  • "I am having trouble navigating lesko help 2 can someone help me"
  • "I still don't know how to navigate through this place. How do I look for the help I need."
  • "I just joined yesterday I'm 54 disabled and I'm really getting lost trying to figure out how to navigate on this site"
  • "can I get help figuring out how to navigate this mass of information on my smart phone?"
  • "I am annoyed at trying to get to the actual giveaway applications for ANYTHING. All I see are several click here links."
  • "Where are the answers to my questions that I have been posting?"

How this was measured

Sampling

The Questions Channel feed cursor turned out to be a base64 unix timestamp, which made it possible to jump to any point in the community's history. The feed was sampled at roughly monthly intervals from October 2022 to August 2026, plus continuous pagination across the most recent five weeks — 1,600 unique posts across 22 monthly strata, rather than a recency-skewed slice.

Labelling

A deliberately high-precision pattern pass produced 212 candidates. Every one was then read and labelled by hand, because automated matching kept mistaking grant requests for support requests — "tax refund", "I'm new here", "my report". Two recall passes over the remaining 1,326 posts recovered 36 more the strict patterns had missed.

Excluded

  • Grant and help-seeking questions — the other 90%
  • Posts by hosts, staff and community managers
  • Announcements, resource shares and social chat

Read the numbers as

  • A sample, not a census — the order is solid, the monthly figures are estimates
  • Questions asked publicly; email and DM support are not visible here
  • Under-counting follow-ups, since comment threads aren't in the search index
