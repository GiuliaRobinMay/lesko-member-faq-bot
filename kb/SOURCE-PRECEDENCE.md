# Which source wins

The community has been rebuilt recently. Older material describes the old way.

## Order of authority

1. **Giulia** — the community architect. Her word is the current state and
   overrides everything below, including anything a host says on a recording.
2. **The instruction apps** (roadmap, call sheet, application follow-up) — current.
3. **leskosupport.com** — partly out of date (still describes the old cancellation flow).
4. **Class transcripts** — hosts can describe the old way, or simply be wrong.
   Useful for member phrasings and for what confuses people. Not authoritative
   on how the platform works.

## Rule

Never change an answer Giulia has confirmed on the strength of a transcript or
another source. If a source disagrees with her, **ask her** — do not assume she
is out of date.

## Corrections already made under this rule

- **Bookmarking**: the bookmark icon on a post exists. A transcript implied only
  the three-dots menu; the icon is the primary route.
- **Signing in**: always the email address used to buy the membership, plus the
  password set when the account was created. A transcript mentioned Google and
  Facebook sign-in — that is not the current onboarding and has been removed.
