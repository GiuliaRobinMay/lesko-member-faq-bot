Fourteen member questions that Mighty Networks documents, checked one by one against what is actually true for Lesko Help members. Five of their articles would send your members to the wrong place.

Ten are now written into the bot in our own words, with no links to their pages. Two are held back waiting on your answer, two are skipped.

The thing that makes half of these dangerous

Mighty Networks writes its help pages for communities that take the money inside Mighty Networks. Lesko Help does not — members pay through ClickBank, Recurly or PayPal, outside the platform entirely.

So every Mighty Networks article about money — cards, billing, cancelling, refunds — describes a screen that is empty or missing for your members. You spotted this with the credit card. It runs deeper: their own documentation says that in a community taking payment externally, a member who leaves loses access immediately rather than at the end of the billing period.

The list below separates the answers we can lift as-is from the ones that need a Lesko correction bolted on before they go anywhere near a member.

Four things only you can answer

Items 6 and 9 are held back until you answer 1 and 4 below. Everything else on this list is already live in the bot.

Per the source rules we set: your word outranks Mighty Networks' documentation, so I have changed nothing on the strength of their pages alone.

  1. Does leaving really kill access immediately?

    Mighty Networks says an external-payment community cuts access the moment a member deactivates, not at the end of the paid period. If that is what actually happens, members should be warned before they click — and the current cancel answer needs a timing line. If your automatic pickup handles it differently, tell me and I will leave the answer as it stands.

  2. What should a member do after changing their email?

    If they change it in the community but the ClickBank or Recurly record still holds the old one, Customer Service cannot match them later. Should the answer tell them to email you so you update the payment record — and is that something Mary handles?

  3. Is there any Lesko route to change a card, other than emailing you?

    Right now the answer would be "go to Recurly, PayPal or ClickBank yourself, or write to us." If Customer Service can just do it, that is a much shorter and kinder answer.

  4. Can anyone buy the membership through the Apple app?

    This decides whether item 9 goes in at all. If every purchase comes through the sales page, I leave Apple refunds out entirely rather than inventing a route that does not exist.
