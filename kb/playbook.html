How do I cancel my subscription or membership?

Sourced from staff replies

There are two ways: cancel it yourself on the Lesko Support site, or email Customer Service and they will do it for you and confirm by reply.

Members ask it like this

  • How do I cancel my subscription?
  • Where is the link to cancel before the 5-day window?
  • I'm changing banks, how do I cancel my paid membership?
  • Cancel membership
  • It is next to impossible to cancel this subscription

The answer — step by step

  1. Self-serve: go to https://leskosupport.com/account-management and scroll to the bottom of the page — the cancellation instructions are there.
  2. Or email LeskoHelp@gmail.com and ask to cancel. Mary Pacey in Customer Service handles cancellations and will reply to confirm it is done.
  3. Before you cancel, download anything shared with you — Call Sheets, reports, spreadsheets. That material stays yours whether you stay or go.
  4. On the $1 / 5-day trial: cancel before the 5 days are up to avoid the next charge.
Hand to a human when. Member says they already emailed and got no reply, or is still being charged after cancelling → hand to Customer Service with the member's email address and dates.

How do I use the AI Grant Researcher?

Sourced from staff replies

Pick your topic, enter your city and state or ZIP, and hit Run Research Tool. Results build up over several minutes, then you can open each one for contact details or export the whole list.

Members ask it like this

  • Does the members AI tool work, I keep getting page not available
  • Your AI doesn't work, I've been trying for a month
  • Where do I find the AI tool?
  • Where did my AI research results go?

The answer — step by step

  1. Open the AI Grant Researcher: https://lesko-help-2.mn.co/spaces/24461105
  2. Select your Topic, then enter your ZIP code or your city and state.
  3. Hit 'Run Research Tool'. Results arrive one or two at a time and a full search can take 10–15 minutes depending on your device and connection.
  4. Click '2: Results' to see what it found in your area.
  5. Hit 'See Details' on any organisation for phone numbers, email addresses, eligibility, funding amounts, and a ready-made outreach message.
  6. Click '3: Export' to download the list to your device as Excel or PDF.
  7. Click 'Clear Report' to run a fresh search for another topic or area.
  8. Want to be taught it live? The AI Workshop runs Fridays, and AI User Skills with Roger runs Wednesdays.

Which app is it and how do I install it?

Sourced from staff replies

The app is Mighty Networks, not a "Lesko" app — which is why searching the store for Lesko finds nothing. Get it via the QR code in your Personal Settings.

Members ask it like this

  • How do I download the app?
  • What is the name of the app that I need to download
  • I can't find the Lesko app on Google Play
  • Can I have the app on my laptop?

The answer — step by step

  1. Click your profile picture in the top right, then 'Personal Settings'.
  2. Scroll down and click 'Download the app' — a QR code appears.
  3. Scan the QR code, then use the button for Google Play or the App Store depending on your device.
  4. Log in on your phone with the same email address and password you use on the computer.
  5. Searching the store for 'Lesko' will not find it — the app is called Mighty Networks.

13

Too many emails and notifications

Sourced from staff replies

Emails and in-app notifications are controlled separately. Emails: unsubscribe at the bottom of the email. Notifications: profile picture → Personal Settings → Notifications.

Members ask it like this

  • How do I unsubscribe from the emails?
  • I don't want to receive so many emails
  • How do I delete all previous notifications?
  • Is there a way to expand the notifications on full screen?

The answer — step by step

  1. For emails: scroll to the bottom of any email you don't want and click 'Unsubscribe'. You may need to do this more than once — you can be signed up through more than one list.
  2. For in-app notifications: click your profile picture → 'Personal Settings' → 'Notifications', then choose the levels you want.
  3. On a phone you can also turn notifications off for the app in your phone's own settings.
  4. Notifications cannot be deleted. Clicking one clears its unread dot, but the list stays.
Watch out. Unsubscribing from emails does NOT cancel your paid membership — those are separate. Members regularly confuse the two.
Sources: S9, S10, S11 🎬 Screen recording: Personal Settings → Notifications, and the unsubscribe link in an email.
